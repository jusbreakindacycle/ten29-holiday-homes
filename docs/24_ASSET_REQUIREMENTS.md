# 24 — Asset Requirements

Exact assets needed from the owner before final launch. Until supplied, the site uses neutral placeholders — never stock tourism photography or images of other properties.

## Images (required for full launch)

| Asset | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Official logo | SVG preferred, or transparent PNG ≥ 1000px wide |
| Exterior images | JPEG/WebP, landscape, ≥ 1920×1280, natural daylight preferred |
| Living-room images | Same as above |
| Bedroom images | One set per distinct bedroom |
| Bathroom images | One set per distinct bathroom |
| Kitchen/dining images | — |
| Parking/entrance image | — |
| Amenity images | Only for amenities that actually exist and work |
| Safety-equipment images | Fire extinguisher, smoke detector, first-aid kit, marked emergency exits |
| Optional property walkthrough video | MP4, ≤ 60s, no music requiring a license unless owned |

**File naming:** `ten29-[area]-[number].jpg`, e.g. `ten29-bedroom-01.jpg`, `ten29-exterior-02.jpg`. Store originals outside the repository; only optimized, owner-approved copies are committed under `public/images/ten29/`.

**Written image-use permission:** a short written confirmation (email is sufficient) that the owner holds rights to each submitted image and approves its use on the website.

## Factual approvals (see [[05_OWNER_APPROVAL_MATRIX]] for the full register)

Resolved by the owner-supplied property description of 2026-07-28 (see
[[27_TEN29_OWNER_FACTUAL_CONFIRMATION]]): room/bed/bath counts and types, air-conditioning
coverage, Wi-Fi availability, kitchen equipment, entertainment, guest amenities, fire
extinguisher and smoke-alarm presence, gated-parking and CCTV existence (not capacity or
locations), the accommodation model, and qualified surroundings/travel-time wording.

Still needed from the owner:

- Approved public address detail (how much of the block/lot to disclose, if any)
- Approved maximum safe occupancy and total-persons limit
- Approved parking capacity (number of vehicles) and CCTV locations/coverage
- Approved capacity, rates, and promotions (currently steward-approved proposals only)
- Approved payment wording and official payment-account holder
- Approved house rules and cancellation policy (currently steward-approved proposals only)
- Approved logo usage terms
- Final Instagram URL and active business/privacy-contact emails
- Final Facebook and Google Maps URLs (both accounts are confirmed to exist; the URLs themselves are not yet supplied)

## Compliance/permit documentation (for full launch confidence, not blocking Option 1 code delivery)

Business permit, barangay clearance, BIR registration, DTI/SEC record where applicable, HOA/subdivision short-stay permission, tourism registration/accreditation, owner/operator authorization, official payment-account holder identity.

None of these are fabricated or assumed. They remain `UNVERIFIED` in the claim registry, and the corresponding public copy stays in its fallback state, until the owner supplies them.
