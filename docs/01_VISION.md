# 01 — Vision

> Make TEN29 Holiday Homes the most trusted and easiest-to-understand direct digital entry point for a holiday-home stay in Biclatan, General Trias, while preserving owner control and growing safely from manual enquiries into a unified booking, payment, and AI-assisted operation.

## What trust means for TEN29

- **Correct location.** The site states General Trias, Biclatan as the actual location and never repositions the property as being in Tagaytay.
- **Authentic owner-approved photos.** No stock tourism imagery, no copied images of Sky Ranch, Picnic Grove, or competing properties.
- **Clear prices and policies.** Rates, deposits, and cancellation terms are stated plainly or transparently marked as pending, never obscured.
- **No fake ratings or fabricated amenities.** Nothing is claimed that has not been verified or owner-approved.
- **Written booking references in later phases.** Every enquiry gets a traceable reference code from Option 1 onward, setting up the booking-reference discipline that Option 2 formalizes.
- **Consistent identity across the website, Facebook, Instagram, and Google Maps.** The website becomes the canonical source of truth that other channels should eventually match.

## Long-term trajectory

Option 1 is the trust foundation. Option 2 adds a real booking and payment ledger without breaking Option 1's routes, content, or data. Option 3 adds AI-assisted Messenger conversation and gateway payments on top of the same foundation. At no point does an upgrade require discarding the public site, the domain, the brand system, or the enquiry records built in Option 1.
