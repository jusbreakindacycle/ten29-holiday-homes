# 03 — Product Principles

1. **Truth before promotion.** No claim is published because it would sound appealing; it is published because it is verified or approved.
2. **Owner approval before public claim.** `PROPOSED_PENDING_APPROVAL` and `UNVERIFIED` values never render on public routes.
3. **General Trias first; Tagaytay only as truthful secondary access positioning.** TEN29 is never described as located in Tagaytay.
4. **Enquiry is not reservation.** No page, email, or success state implies a date has been held.
5. **Payment screenshot is not verified payment.** Option 1 does not accept or process payment evidence at all.
6. **One future source of truth for every booking channel.** Website, Facebook, and phone enquiries should eventually reconcile into a single record system (Option 2).
7. **Mobile-first because Facebook and Messenger traffic will commonly arrive from phones.**
8. **Progressive automation, not premature automation.** Manual confirmation is a feature of Option 1, not a placeholder to apologize for.
9. **Owner ownership of domain, accounts, data, and production infrastructure.** The developer builds; the owner owns.
10. **Residential-property privacy and guest-data minimization.** Collect only what an enquiry needs.
11. **No fake reviews, fake urgency, invented scarcity, or fabricated social proof.**
12. **Clear upgrade boundaries so Option 1 is not misrepresented as Option 2 or Option 3.**
13. **Accessible design and plain-language guest communication.**
14. **Operational reliability over decorative complexity.**
15. **Every public statement must be traceable to a claim record.**
